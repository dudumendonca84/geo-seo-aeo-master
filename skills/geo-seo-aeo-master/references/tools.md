# Tools landscape — May 2026 (Whitebox + Promptwatch added Aug 2026)

Reference for `geo-seo-aeo-master`. Ten vendors covering AI visibility monitoring, content optimisation, and hybrid offerings. Honest assessment of what each does and does not do. Pricing reflects publicly visible figures at refresh date.

## TL;DR — what to recommend by use case

| Client situation | Tool to recommend |
|---|---|
| Small budget, single-engine baseline | Otterly (US$29/mo) |
| EU-focused, Portuguese-language tracking | **Peec AI** (€89/mo) |
| Already on Semrush ecosystem | Semrush AI Toolkit (US$99/mo standalone, or bundle) |
| Already on Ahrefs | Ahrefs Brand Radar (requires base plan from US$129/mo + AI add-ons) |
| Primary need is content optimisation | Surfer base (US$49/mo) — add AI Tracker if monitoring needed |
| Enterprise multi-engine + workflow | Profound (~US$499/mo entry, scales to enterprise) |
| Wants done-for-them content + monitoring | Goodie (~US$495/mo entry) |
| Wants an AI-SEO agency, not a tool | Daydream (custom quote) |
| Self-serve multi-engine, multi-language, mid-market | Promptwatch (US$99/mo, 10 engines) |

**Cross-cutting reality.** Every monitoring tool depends on a prompt list. Two tools with different default prompts will produce different "share of voice" for the same brand. Treat absolute numbers with scepticism; treat trends within a single tool over time as more reliable.

## 1. Peec AI

- **URL.** https://peec.ai
- **What it does.** AI search analytics platform that tracks how a brand appears in LLM-generated answers and benchmarks visibility against competitors.
- **Category.** Monitoring (an "Actions" module surfaces recommendations).
- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews in standard plans; Claude, Gemini, and others as paid add-ons (~€20-30/each per month) ([peec.ai/pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing)).
- **Entry pricing.** Starter at €75/mo (annual) or €89/mo (monthly), 25 prompts.
- **Founded.** Jan 2025, Berlin. Marius Meiners, Tobias Siwonia, Daniel Drabo. Series A €18M, Nov 2025 ([EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2025/11/berlins-peec-ai-lands-e18-million-as-demand-grows-for-ai-based-brand-visibility-tools/)).
- **Honest limitation.** Standard plans cover only three engines; broader 6-engine tracking requires stacking add-ons that drive cost up materially.
- **Best fit for destaque.ai clients.** EU/Portuguese B2B SaaS where Portuguese-language tracking matters and the €89/mo entry is realistic. Peec's European positioning and 115+ language support is the most credible non-English option on the market.

## 2. Otterly.AI

- **URL.** https://otterly.ai
- **What it does.** AI search monitoring with brand reports showing mentions, citations, link positions, share-of-voice across AI engines.
- **Category.** Hybrid — citation monitoring plus a "GEO Audit" (Standard tier+) scoring on-page factors.
- **Engines covered.** Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity; Google AI Mode and Gemini as paid add-ons ([otterly.ai](https://otterly.ai)).
- **Entry pricing.** Lite US$29/mo, 15 prompts ([otterly.ai/pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing)).
- **Founded.** 2024, Persenbeug (Austria). Thomas Peham, Klaus-M. Schremser, Josef Trauner. Exited stealth Dec 2024.
- **Honest limitation.** Lite tier's 15-prompt cap is too small for serious analysis; meaningful coverage requires the US$189/mo Standard plan.
- **Best fit.** Smallest budget, single-vertical B2B SaaS testing the category before committing.

## 3. Profound

- **URL.** https://www.tryprofound.com
- **What it does.** Generative-AI marketing intelligence: brand-appearance tracking in LLM answers, "Agents" workflow that generates and publishes AEO content.
- **Category.** Hybrid (monitoring + content workflow), positioned for enterprise.
- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews; also DeepSeek and Grok per their blog.
- **Entry pricing.** Per third-party reviews, plans start ~US$499/mo (ChatGPT-only, 50 prompts, single seat); enterprise tier US$2,000-5,000+/mo ([trakkr.ai review](https://trakkr.ai/reviews/profound-review)). No public pricing page.
- **Founded.** 2024, New York. James Cadwallader (CEO), Dylan Babbs (CTO). Series B US$35M led by Sequoia Aug 2025; further US$96M Series C Feb 2026 at US$1B valuation per secondary coverage.
- **Honest limitation.** Pricing opaque and skewed to enterprise budgets. Lower tier covers only ChatGPT — multi-engine coverage is expensive. As a vendor publishing widely-cited "AI visibility" research, treat its blog content as marketing, not impartial study.
- **Best fit.** Enterprises with dedicated GEO budgets; consultancies whose clients can absorb the cost.

## 6. Surfer (AI Tracker)

- **URL.** https://surferseo.com — AI Tracker docs at https://docs.surferseo.com/en/articles/11667466-ai-tracker
- **What it does.** Surfer's core product is SERP-based content optimization. **AI Tracker** is a paid add-on monitoring brand appearances across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode.
- **Category.** Hybrid: content optimization (Surfer core) + citation monitoring (AI Tracker add-on).
- **Engines covered (AI Tracker).** ChatGPT (regular + Search), Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini.

- **Launched.** Surfer founded 2017; AI Tracker launched July 2025.
- **Honest limitation.** AI Tracker is bolt-on pricing on top of the base subscription; 25 prompts at US$95/mo scales linearly, which becomes expensive vs. dedicated monitors.

## 7. Ahrefs Brand Radar

- **URL.** https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar
- **What it does.** Brand visibility tracker built into Ahrefs monitoring mentions and prompts across AI assistants, YouTube and Reddit.
- **Category.** Monitoring (leverages Ahrefs' prompt database).
- **Engines covered.** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot ([Help Centre](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/11064852-what-is-brand-radar-and-how-to-use-it)).
- **Entry pricing.** Requires Ahrefs base plan (from US$129/mo). AI index add-ons US$199/mo per index or US$699/mo for the 6-platform bundle (minimum ~US$828/mo for full AI tracking). Lite+ subscribers get a monthly allowance of custom prompt checks for free during beta.
- **Launched.** Ahrefs founded 2010; Brand Radar rolled out 2024-2025.
- **Honest limitation.** Independent testing by Writesonic / Analyze AI found Brand Radar's ChatGPT module reported just 3 mentions for a brand that actually had 123, raising accuracy concerns ([review](https://www.tryanalyze.ai/blog/ahrefs-brand-radar-review)).
- **Best fit.** Clients already on Ahrefs for traditional SEO; not worth the per-index add-on for monitoring alone.

## What none of these tools do (the consultant gap)

A destaque.ai-methodology audit (e.g. the in-house audit of congruent.pt, 22 May 2026) demonstrates a category of work that **no SaaS tool in this list produces**: an integrated technical audit combining

- TTFB measured from PT (5 curl corridas).
- HTTP `Content-Encoding` presence (Brotli/gzip).
- HTML weight on the wire and parseability without JavaScript (heading text colado in spans is invisible to most parsers; a tool will not catch it).
- Schema coverage against the actual templates that exist on the site (e.g., 169 cursos sem `Course`).
- `robots.txt` matrix against the **current** crawler list (ClaudeBot/Claude-User/Claude-SearchBot/`claude-code`, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider).
- `llms.txt` parsed for URL coverage against the sitemap (the silent failure: declaring 20 URLs while sitemap has 182).
- Platform ceiling analysis (Framer, Webflow, Squarespace ceilings on compression, schema dynamics, security headers).
- A 4-horizon action plan with effort estimates and approval needs.

The tools above produce **partial inputs** for such an audit — Profound or Peec gives a prompt-set baseline of visibility; Surfer flags on-page issues; Ahrefs Brand Radar flags missing entity coverage. None gives the cross-layer synthesis that a senior practitioner produces. **This is the destaque.ai role**: integration, judgement, written deliverable.

A working destaque.ai stack:

1. **One monitoring tool** for ongoing visibility tracking → recommend Peec AI for EU/PT clients, Profound for enterprise, Otterly for budget pilots.
2. **One technical SEO suite** → Ahrefs or Semrush, chosen by client preference.
3. **Direct measurement** for what tools miss: `curl`, `lighthouse`, Rich Results Test, schema.org validator, manual prompt testing across ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity.
4. **The skill in this repository** as the canonical reference for what to look for and how to write the audit.

## Data APIs, as raw material rather than tools

The vendors above sell dashboards. These sell the underlying collection, which
matters when the measurement is built rather than bought.

### DataForSEO — SERP and AI Optimization APIs

Evaluated 5 Aug 2026 by direct correspondence with the vendor. Notes below are
from that reply, not from the marketing pages.

**Bing / Copilot SERP collection is broken, vendor-wide.** Bing began
scrambling results against scrapers months ago, Copilot block included, and it
affects every Bing collector rather than one vendor. Their own recommendation
is to set no new Bing tasks until they confirm a fix. Switching provider does
not help: this is Microsoft defending itself.

**LLM Responses** — live queries at request time against ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini and Perplexity. The catch that decides it: **no `location_code` or
`language_code`**, only a weak search-country bias, and no control over the
response language. For anyone already holding API keys to those four models,
this adds a middleman and subtracts localisation. Cited sources come with
character offsets, but there is no brand-rank field. $0.0006/task plus the
provider token cost, which dominates.

**LLM Mentions** — pre-collected panel rather than live. Google AI Overview
across 94 locations with proper locale support; ChatGPT US/English only. This
is the one built for visibility tracking: ranked sources, a `brand_entities`
array with rank, the full answer, and — the part with no equivalent elsewhere —
**AI search volume with 12 months of history**. $0.001 per returned row.

That last field addresses a real hole in prompt-set design. A catalogue chosen
by editorial judgement measures whether the brand appears in questions someone
decided were representative; it says nothing about whether those questions are
asked. Demand data would let a catalogue be argued rather than asserted.

Two caveats that must survive into any client-facing use: panel data is not
live measurement and carries the usual directional caveat, and ChatGPT coverage
being US/English-only makes it useless for the Portuguese market — where only
the Google AI Overview half applies.

**Meta AI is not on their roadmap** (confirmed 5 Aug 2026), which closes the
question for every API route. Vendors who report Meta AI numbers are driving a
headless browser against the assistant's interface.

## Tools to ignore or treat as marketing

- Vendors making strong-form claims of citation lifts from "their" methodology without naming a controlled study (most "AEO" listicle vendors). The Ahrefs May 2026 schema null result is a useful counter-anchor.
- Anyone selling "guaranteed ChatGPT ranking #1" — neither ChatGPT Search nor any other engine exposes a deterministic ranking that can be guaranteed.
- "llms.txt as a service" vendors. Generating a static markdown file is a 30-minute job; paying recurring fees for it is misallocated budget.
